# Keeping the school website accurate

A guide for the school office

The website is the first thing many parents see. This guide explains what to send us so it stays
correct, and what we will never put on it without your say-so.

## The one rule

**We publish only what the school has confirmed.** If we are not sure of something, the website says
what is missing instead of guessing. You will see short grey notes such as "will be added once the
school confirms". Those notes are a prompt to send us the information.

## Sending a notice

Send us four things:

1. A short title, for example "Parent–teacher meeting".
2. The date.
3. The exact text you want parents to read.
4. Who approved it (Principal, Chairman or office).

We will put it on the Notices page. Please do not send a notice that has not been approved.

Contact for updates: **[to be filled in: name, phone or email of the person maintaining the site]**

## Sending photographs

- Take photographs with the phone's normal camera, held sideways (landscape), in good daylight.
- Send the **original photo file**, not a screenshot. Screenshots include the phone's buttons and are
  blurry. If you use WhatsApp, send it "as a document" so it is not squashed.
- Check the school signboard in the picture. It must read **Uma Bharti**, spelled with "Bharti".
- **Students:** please do not send photographs where a child can be recognised unless their parent or
  guardian has agreed in writing. If in doubt, photograph the building, the grounds or the work, not the faces.

## Sending results

Take the figures from the official result sheet and send, for each class:

- how many students appeared,
- how many passed,
- how many achieved merit.

We do **not** publish the names, photographs or marks of individual students unless each parent or
guardian has given written permission and the school has checked the figures against its records.

## Enquiries from parents

When a parent fills in the enquiry form on the website, the message arrives in the school's email
inbox (**umabhartischool@gmail.com**) once the form has been switched on. Please check that inbox every
day, and call the parent back on the number they gave. The form asks only for the parent's name, phone
number, the class they are interested in and an optional message. It deliberately does not ask for a
child's name or date of birth.

## What we are still waiting for from the school

These are the gaps parents can currently see on the website:

- The Principal's name, a formal photograph and a short message.
- A formal photograph of the Chairman, Mr. Randhir Singh Chauhan, and a short message from him.
- The number of students and teachers, and the sections in each class.
- Which facilities exist on campus (library, laboratories, playground, transport and so on), with photographs.
- School timings, working days and the holiday calendar.
- The full admission process and the list of documents parents must bring.
- A decision on whether to publish the fee structure.
- Which phone number is on WhatsApp, if the school wants a "chat on WhatsApp" button.
- The school's Facebook page address, if it has one.
- The HBSE affiliation number and the "Permanent Recognised" certificate number.
- A clear copy of the school crest on a transparent background, or the original design file.
- Better photographs of the buildings: full-resolution originals, with the signboard spelled "Bharti".
- Confirmation of whether the white block and the peach block are two buildings of the same campus.
- The exact events and positions for the sports results, checked against the school's records.
- Whether the admission details on the site (the test on 8 February 2026 and the 2026–27 session)
  are still current, or should be replaced with newer dates.
