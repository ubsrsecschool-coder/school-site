Our Story

A neighbourhood school with a twenty-five year record.

Uma Bharti Senior Secondary School has served families in Bhora Kalan, Gurugram since 1999. We teach in English, follow the CBSE-pattern curriculum under HBSE affiliation, and take students from their first day in Nursery through to Class XII.
